FlypBook Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

The short version

FlypBook is a free reading app. We collect as little as possible, we never sell your data, and you can delete your account and everything tied to it at any time from your profile.

Not for children under 13

FlypBook accounts are not for children under 13. If the birthday entered at sign-up is under 13, no account is created and nothing is sent to us. Your birthday is never stored. We only record that an account holder confirmed they are 13 or older. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

What we collect (only if you make an account)

  • Account: your email address, username, password (stored securely by our login provider, never visible to us), and a "13 or older" flag.
  • Preferences: the kinds of stories you pick as favorites.
  • Activity: stories you like, share, finish, or skip, your quiz answers, points, streaks, and reading level. These personalize your For You feed.
  • Requests: story ideas you submit, and notifications about them.
  • Reports: stories you report and any reason you give.

Without an account, you can read every story. Your settings (like narration speed) are saved only on your device.

How it's used

  • To run your account, points, streaks, and personalized feed.
  • To write requested stories. Requests are sent to an AI service to generate a story, and the finished story is shared with everyone, credited to your username.
  • To keep FlypBook safe. Every story and request is checked for unsafe content, and reported stories are hidden for review.

AI-generated content

Every story on FlypBook is written by AI and labeled as AI-generated. Narration is made with an AI voice. Characters and events are fictional.

Who handles data for us

  • Supabase: our database and login provider.
  • Groq: the AI service that writes stories (it receives story requests, not your email or account details).
  • GitHub: hosts the app and runs the story job.
  • jsDelivr: delivers some open-source code the app uses.
  • Google Fonts: delivers the caption fonts (your browser downloads them; no account info is shared).

Background clips come from Pixabay and are credited in the app. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

Open your profile and tap Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and all personal data listed above. Stories you requested stay public but no longer show your username.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/FlypBook/flypbook.github.io/issues.
